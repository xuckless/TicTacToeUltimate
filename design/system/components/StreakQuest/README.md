# StreakQuest

The streak flame pill (top bar) and the daily quest card (Home).

- **Consumer provides:** the streak day count and whether today is already secured. Quests take a title plus progress and goal.
- The streak counts days with at least 1 finished game. A rewarded ad grants a streak freeze.
- The daily quests are Win 1, Win 3 and Win 5. A completed quest shows a ✓ in `success`.
