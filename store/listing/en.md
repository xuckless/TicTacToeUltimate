## Description
Classic tic-tac-toe ends in a draw. This one never does.

You only ever have three pieces on the board. Place a fourth and your oldest one vanishes, so every move changes the board and every game ends with a winner. Easy to learn, surprisingly deep, and fast: most matches take about a minute.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Casual: quick matches on a sudden-death clock that speeds up as the game goes on.
• Ranked Arena: lines shatter and deal damage. Knock out your opponent's health bar to climb from Bronze to Master.
• Old school: plain tic-tac-toe when you just want the classic.
• Unlimited: no clock, think as long as you like.
• Daily: one move a day, up to ten games at once.
• Pass & Play: two players on one device, works offline.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
• Send a link or a room code and play in seconds.
• Add friends, chat, challenge them and watch their live games.
• Throw emotes at your opponent (or mute theirs).

KEEP COMING BACK
• Daily quests and streaks, with Diamonds to earn.
• Seasons, tiers and leaderboards.
• Replays of every game, plus a perfect-play review that shows where it was won and lost.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
• Ratings use Glicko-2 and matchmaking pairs you with players at your level.
• Fair-play checks and moderation keep games and chat clean; report anyone who breaks the rules.
