Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What we collect

  • Guests: a random device identifier stored in your browser, your generated guest name, and your games.
  • Accounts: your email address (used only to sign in), your username, country and avatar if you choose them, your games, ratings, streaks and quests.
  • Social features: your friends list, blocks and direct messages with mutual friends.
  • Technical data: connection timing used to keep game clocks fair, and standard server logs.
  • Usage statistics: which screens you open, sessions, matches you join and finish, and which ads were shown, with your app version, platform, language, country (from your connection) and, if you arrived through one of our links, that link's campaign name. We collect these ourselves (no third-party analytics, no cookies) and keep the raw events for 14 days; after that only daily totals remain.
  • Crash and bug reports: when the app hits an error it sends the error details, the last screens and buttons you used (never your messages or what you typed), recent app logs, your app version and device model. If you send a bug report you can include a screenshot; screenshots are deleted after 90 days.
  • Moderation: reports you make or receive, chat messages that our filters or a report flag, and a device identifier so bans also apply to the device.

How we use it

To run matches, keep ratings and leaderboards, show your history and replays, prevent abuse, and keep the game working. We do not sell your personal data.

Moderation and fair play

Direct messages and usernames are checked automatically. A word filter blocks abusive messages before delivery. Messages the filter can't judge, and messages someone reports, are checked by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude), which receives only that text; Anthropic does not use it to train models. Abusive messages are hidden and can lead to warnings and chat mutes. Bans are always decided by a person, and you can appeal any mute or ban once from the app. To keep games fair we compare games with perfect play; accounts that look engine-assisted may be matched only with similar players or computer opponents.

Advertising

On the website we use Google AdSense, and in the iOS and Android apps Google AdMob, to show ads outside of matches. The mobile apps may use your device's advertising identifier (with your permission on iOS via App Tracking Transparency) and the ad SDK may collect diagnostics such as device model and app version. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Storage and services

Accounts, game data, usage statistics and crash reports are stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). The website is served through Cloudflare and the game server runs on Google Cloud. Chat moderation uses Anthropic. Technical details of crashes (error, stack trace, app version, device model, the screens involved) may be copied into our GitHub issue tracker so we can fix them; your name, email, messages and bug-report text are never copied there.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Your choices

You can delete your account and data at any time inside the app or website: Settings → Delete my account (how it works). You can also email us and we will do it for you within 7 days. Clearing your browser storage removes your guest identity.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the app or on the website and tap Delete my account…, then confirm. This happens immediately:

  • Your email, username, avatar, country, device identifier, ratings, streak, quests, friends, friend requests, blocks and messages are deleted.
  • Your sign-in is removed, so the email can no longer log in to this account.
  • Finished games stay in your opponents' history and replays, with your seat shown as "Deleted player".

Prefer email? Write to hello@alidirect.dev from the address you signed up with and we delete the account within 7 days.
