Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

What we collect

  • Guests: a random device identifier stored in your browser, your generated guest name, and your games.
  • Accounts: your email address (used only to sign in and reset your password), your username, country and avatar if you choose them, your games, ratings, streaks and quests.
  • Social features: your friends list, blocks and direct messages with mutual friends.
  • Technical data: connection timing used to keep game clocks fair, and standard server logs.

How we use it

To run matches, keep ratings and leaderboards, show your history and replays, prevent abuse, and keep the game working. We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads outside of matches. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Storage and services

Accounts and game data are stored with Supabase (database and authentication). The website is served through Cloudflare and the game server runs on Google Cloud.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Your choices

You can delete your account and data at any time by contacting us. Clearing your browser storage removes your guest identity.
