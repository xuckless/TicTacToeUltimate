How to play

Tic Tac Toe Ultimate is the tic-tac-toe you know with one twist that changes everything: each player can only have three pieces on the board.

The rules

  1. X moves first, then players alternate placing a piece on an empty cell of the 3×3 board.
  2. Once you already have three pieces down, placing a fourth makes your own oldest piece vanish. The game shows it faded so you always know which one is next.
  3. You can't place on the cell your piece just vacated on that same turn.
  4. Any three of your pieces in a row, column or diagonal win immediately.

Because pieces keep vanishing, the board never fills up. There are no stalemates: every game ends with a winner.

Game modes

Ranked Arena. Lines don't end the game. They shatter and deal damage to your opponent's health bar (125 HP). Score on consecutive turns to build a combo, and damage doubles in the last 30 seconds. Knock your opponent out, or have more health when the 2-minute round ends. Wins move you up a rated ladder from Bronze to Master.

Casual. Classic Ultimate rules on a sudden-death clock: 15 seconds a move, shrinking as the game goes on. Unrated.

Old school. Plain tic-tac-toe where pieces never vanish, for when you want the original.

Unlimited. No clock at all. Take your time.

Daily. One move a day, up to ten games at once, with its own rating.

Pass & Play. Two players on one phone or computer. Works offline.

Tips

  • Watch the faded piece. Your next placement removes it, so a line that depends on it won't hold.
  • Threats that survive your own vanishing piece are the strongest.
  • In ranked play the pie rule keeps things fair: after X's first move, O may swap sides.
  • Guided mode (Settings) highlights threats in casual games while you learn.
